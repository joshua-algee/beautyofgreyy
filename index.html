About Beautyofgreyy

Hi, I'm Miranda Grey Yax.

I have had a love for photography ever since I was a little girl. Over the last decade, I have been honing my skills and becoming more confident in my art.

Being able to capture the beauty of each unique human I meet is what makes it worth it.

The name Beautyofgreyy is deeply tied to who I am. "Grey" is my middle name, and the second "Y" is a nod to my last name, Yax. Just like the yin-yang represented in my logo, life is rarely perfectly black and white. The beauty exists in the in-between, in the grey.
