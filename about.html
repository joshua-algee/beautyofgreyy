About

About Me

Hi, I'm Miranda Grey Yax.

I have had a love for photography ever since I was a little girl. Over the last decade, I have been honing my skills and becoming more confident in my art.

Being able to capture the beauty of each unique human I meet is what makes it worth it.

My goal is to create photographs that feel authentic to you while highlighting the little moments, details, and emotions that make each person unique.

The Meaning Behind the Name

Why Beautyofgreyy?

The name Beautyofgreyy is deeply tied to who I am. "Grey" is my middle name, and the second "Y" is a nod to my last name, Yax.

Just like the yin-yang represented in my logo, life is rarely perfectly balanced between black and white. The beauty exists in the in-between, in the grey.

My goal as your photographer is to highlight the beauty in your world.

Your Session

What to expect

I want your session to feel comfortable, collaborative, and true to you. You do not need to arrive knowing exactly how to pose or what every photo should look like.

01

Planning the vibe

We'll talk about the kind of photos you want, the mood of your session, outfits, colors, and inspiration. Pinterest boards and reference photos are always welcome.

02

Choosing a location

I'll help you think through locations that fit the style of your shoot, whether you're looking for something natural, urban, creative, casual, or polished.

03

During the shoot

You don't need to worry about knowing what to do in front of the camera. I'll help guide posing, movement, placement, and small adjustments throughout the session.
