# Beautyofgreyy brand

## Identity

- **Business**: Beautyofgreyy Photography — photographer **Miranda Grey Yax**. Domain `beautyofgreyy.com`.
- **Name meaning** (keep accurate): "Grey" is her middle name; the second "y" nods to her last name, Yax. Logo is a yin-yang.
- **Core idea / tagline**: "Beauty in the in-between." Life isn't black and white — the beauty is in the grey.
- **Services**: portrait (individuals/pairs), couples, lifestyle, graduation, creative, events, and commercial (restaurants, cafés, retail, small businesses, brands; product imagery).

## Facts: source of truth

Prices, package contents, turnaround times and travel-fee policy live **only** in `pricing.html` and `commercial.html`. Read them there; never invent, round or "improve" numbers, and change them only when the user gives new figures. Bio facts live in `about.html`.

## Voice

First person from Miranda, warm, personal, unpretentious and reassuring ("You do not need to arrive knowing exactly how to pose"). Short sentences. No corporate jargon, hype, or stock-photography clichés. Commercial copy is a bit more practical and process-focused but still personal.

## Visual identity (current — open to redesign)

- Warm neutral palette rooted in "grey": tokens on `:root` in `style.css` (`--background #f7f3ee`, `--background-alt #eee7df`, `--text #2d2926`, `--muted #70665f`, `--border #d7cdc4`, `--dark #292522`).
- Serif typography (Georgia stack), editorial feel, generous whitespace, photography first.
- The redesign may evolve palette/type, but should stay calm, warm, tonal and photo-led — grey as a concept, not dull grey UI. Ask before changing the name, tagline or logo concept.

## Conversion priorities

1. Every page offers a clear path to `booking.html` ("Book Now" in nav + contextual CTAs).
2. Show starting prices early (portraits from the mini session price, commercial hourly rate) to pre-qualify visitors.
3. Photos sell the work: large, fast-loading, real images with descriptive `alt` text.
4. Keep the booking form short; required fields: name, phone, shoot type, possible dates.
5. Reassure: what to expect, turnaround times, friendly tone.
